# TheRealExpenseTracker_PrivacyPolicy
The Real Expense Tracker is built so that your financial information stays yours. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

What we collect:

Nothing. The app has no accounts and no sign-up. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it has no servers of its own. It does not connect to your bank, and it never sends your information to us or to anyone else.

Where your data is stored:
• On your device. Your bills, balances, pay schedule, accounts and goals are stored only on your device.

• In your private iCloud (optional). If you're signed in to iCloud, the app syncs your data between your own devices through your private iCloud database. That data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account, and we can't see or access it. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

• Widgets read the same data on your device to show your figures on the Home and Lock Screens.

Permissions the app may ask for:

Each one is optional. You can decline it or turn it off later in Settings.

• Notifications: to remind you about paydays, bills and low balances. The reminders are scheduled on your device.

• Reminders: to add your bills to the Reminders app, if you turn this on. The app only reads and writes the reminders it creates.

• Face ID / Touch ID: to lock the app. Apple handles the authentication, and the app never receives your face or fingerprint data.

Purchases:

Expense Tracker Plus is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. We receive no payment details or personal information, only confirmation that the purchase is unlocked.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data:

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove synced data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage on your iPhone or iPad, or to System Settings → [your name] → iCloud on your Mac, and delete the app's data there.

Changes to this policy:

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
